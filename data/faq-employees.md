# Employee FAQ

**How do I reset my core banking password?** Use the self-service portal, or log a ticket with
the IT Service Desk if locked out.

**How many days do I have to submit an expense claim?** Expense claims must be submitted within
30 days of the expense being incurred.

**Who approves leave?** Your line manager, through the HR system.

**Can I access a customer's account to help a friend?** No. Accessing accounts without a
legitimate business reason is a disciplinary offence (see the Code of Conduct).
