# HR Policy: Code of Conduct

All employees must act with integrity and protect the confidentiality of customer and group
information. Sharing customer data outside approved systems, or accessing accounts without a
legitimate business reason, is a serious disciplinary offence that may result in dismissal.

Gifts from customers or suppliers exceeding US$50 in value must be declared to the Ethics
Office. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed in writing. Outside employment requires prior
written approval from Human Resources.
