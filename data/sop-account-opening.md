# Standard Operating Procedure: Account Opening

To open a personal account, a customer must provide:

1. A valid national identity document or passport.
2. Proof of residence not older than three months (e.g., a utility bill).
3. Proof of income or source of funds for higher-risk products.

The account-opening officer performs CDD and sanctions screening (see AML policies) before the
account is activated. Records must be captured in the core banking system and original
documents verified against copies. Incomplete files must not be activated.
