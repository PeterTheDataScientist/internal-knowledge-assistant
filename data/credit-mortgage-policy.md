# Credit Policy: Mortgages & Home Loans

Home loans (mortgages) are secured against residential property.

- Maximum loan-to-value (LTV) ratio: **80%** of the professionally assessed property value.
- Maximum tenure: **300 months** (25 years).
- The property must be insured, with Contoso noted as loss payee, for the life of the loan.

A registered valuation and proof of title are required before disbursement. Mortgage approvals
above US$100,000 require Credit Committee sign-off.
