# AML: Sanctions Screening

All customers and counterparties are screened against applicable sanctions lists, including
United Nations, OFAC, and other lists mandated by the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe, at onboarding
and continuously thereafter.

A confirmed sanctions match must be frozen and escalated to the Compliance department
immediately; no funds may be released. The bank does not process transactions for sanctioned
individuals or entities. Screening results and their disposition must be logged and retained
for a minimum of five years.
