# Products: Deposit Accounts

Contoso Bank offers savings and current (cheque) accounts for individuals and businesses.

- **Contoso Savings Account**: no monthly maintenance fee; interest paid quarterly.
- **Contoso Current Account**: monthly maintenance fee applies; includes a cheque book and debit card.
- Foreign currency accounts (Nostro) are available in USD.

Accounts can be opened at any branch or through the Contoso Online app subject to identity verification.
Minimum opening balances vary by product and are published on the branch tariff sheet.
