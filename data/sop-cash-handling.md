# Standard Operating Procedure: Cash Handling

Tellers operate under dual control for vault access. Individual teller cash holdings must not
exceed the branch teller limit set by Branch Operations; excess cash must be lodged to the
vault promptly.

All cash differences, whether overages or shortages, must be reported to the Branch Manager on
the same day and recorded in the differences register. Suspected fraud must be escalated to
Loss Control and Compliance immediately.
