# Products: Contoso Family Funeral Plan

The Contoso Family Funeral Plan, underwritten by Contoso Life Assurance, provides a cash benefit on the
death of a covered life to help families meet funeral costs.

The plan covers the principal member, one spouse, and dependent children, with optional
extended-family cover. A waiting period of six months applies to death from natural causes;
there is no waiting period for accidental death. Premiums are payable monthly and the policy
lapses if premiums are unpaid for three consecutive months.
