# AML / KYC Policy

Contoso applies a risk-based approach to anti-money-laundering (AML) in line with RBZ and Financial
Intelligence Unit (FIU) requirements.

Customer Due Diligence (CDD) is required for all new accounts. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
applies to high-risk customers, including Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and customers from
high-risk jurisdictions.

Any suspicious transaction must be escalated to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO),
who files a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) with the FIU **within 3 business days** of
detection. Employees must never "tip off" a customer that an STR has been or may be filed.
Transaction monitoring thresholds are configured in the core banking system and reviewed
quarterly.
