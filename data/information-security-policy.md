# Information Security Policy

Access to systems follows least-privilege and need-to-know principles. Multi-factor
authentication is mandatory for all staff accessing banking systems remotely.

Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are never to be shared. Staff must lock
workstations when unattended. Security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations
Centre without delay. Production data must not be copied to personal devices or unapproved
cloud services.
