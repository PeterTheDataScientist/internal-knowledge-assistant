# Products: Cards

Contoso issues debit cards linked to current and savings accounts, and credit cards to qualifying
customers.

Debit cards work on local POS and ATM networks. Credit cards have a minimum income requirement
and are subject to a credit assessment under the Credit Policy. Lost or stolen cards must be
blocked immediately by the customer through the Contoso Online app, the 24-hour card centre, or any
branch. Card replacement fees apply per the tariff sheet.
