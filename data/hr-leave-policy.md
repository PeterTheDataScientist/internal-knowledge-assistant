# HR Policy: Leave

Permanent employees are entitled to **22 working days of annual (vacation) leave** per
calendar year. Annual leave accrues monthly and a maximum of 10 unused days may be carried
over into the following year; days beyond that are forfeited.

Sick leave: up to 90 days on full pay and a further 90 days on half pay in any 12-month
period, subject to a medical certificate for absences exceeding two consecutive days.

Maternity leave is 98 calendar days. Compassionate leave is up to 5 days per event. Leave
must be approved by the employee's line manager through the HR system before it is taken.
