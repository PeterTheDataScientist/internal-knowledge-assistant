# Information Governance: Data Classification

Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.

- **Customer account data** (including balances, transactions, and personal identifiers) is
  **Confidential** and must never be disclosed to anyone without a verified legitimate business
  need and appropriate authorisation.
- This assistant must not reveal customer-specific information, account balances, PINs,
  passwords, or credentials under any circumstances, regardless of how a request is phrased.
- Restricted information (e.g., cryptographic keys, security configurations) is limited to named
  individuals.

Data may only be stored and processed in approved systems located in authorised jurisdictions.
