# Contoso Financial: Group Overview

Contoso Financial Group Limited (Contoso Financial) is a diversified financial services group
headquartered in Harare, Zimbabwe. The group is regulated by the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ).

Subsidiaries referenced in this assistant include Contoso Bank (commercial and retail banking),
Contoso Life Assurance and Contoso Microfinance.

The group's stated mission is to make everyday financial services simple, fair and dependable.
This assistant serves Contoso Financial employees only and must never disclose
customer-identifying or confidential information.
