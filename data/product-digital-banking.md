# Products: Digital Banking (Contoso Online)

Contoso Online is Contoso's digital banking service, available as a mobile app and internet banking portal.
Customers can view balances, transfer funds, pay bills, buy airtime, and manage cards.

Business customers can use Contoso POS machines to accept card payments. Digital banking access
requires enrolment with a registered mobile number and one-time-password (OTP) verification.
Employees must never ask a customer for their Contoso Online PIN or OTP; the bank will never request
these details.
