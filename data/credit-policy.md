# Credit Policy: Personal Lending

Personal (unsecured) loans are available to salaried customers who have held an active Contoso Bank
account for at least three months.

- Maximum unsecured personal loan tenure: **60 months**.
- The total monthly loan repayment must not exceed **40% of the customer's net monthly salary**
  (debt-service-to-income limit).
- Loans above US$20,000 require approval by the Credit Committee; below that, a branch credit
  officer may approve within delegated limits.

All applications require an affordability assessment and a credit bureau check. Loans to
employees follow the same policy with an additional HR sign-off.
